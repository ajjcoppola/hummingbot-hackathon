# US venue gate (do not bypass)

You asked Perplexity how a US citizen can race. The intersection of **official eligible venues** and **ToS you can keep** is small.

| Venue | Official race? | US person? | Use? |
|-------|----------------|------------|------|
| Orca (Solana CLMM) | Yes (sponsor) | Self-custody DEX | **Yes — this entry** |
| Meteora (Solana DLMM) | Yes (sponsor) | Self-custody DEX | Backup team only |
| XRPL DEX | Yes (sponsor) | On-chain CLOB | Legal, but not this repo |
| Binance Global | Eligible list | ToS bans US | **No** |
| Gate | Sponsor | ToS bans US | **No** |
| Bitget | Sponsor | ToS bans US | **No** |
| Hyperliquid | Eligible list | Frontend ToS restricts US | **No** |
| Derive | Sponsor | ToS: no US persons | **No** |
| Coinbase / Kraken / Gemini | Not a Cup sponsor | KYC CEX OK | Fine for learning, **not scored** |

VPN around a CEX geo-block is a ToS violation, a prize/KYC risk, and a disqualification risk. Do not do it.

Botcamp **funds the $800 finals account** in their Docker. That does not make a banned venue legal for you.

DEX trades are still **IRS taxable**. Use a dedicated wallet. This is not legal advice.
